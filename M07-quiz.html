RRecap — what to remember

  • Dynamics 365 ships Copilots for finance, supply chain, customer experience, and service.
  • M365 Copilot for Sales and for Service are configurable, not custom-built.
  • Power Platform AI Hub centralizes AI assets across the tenant.
  • Finance and Operations agent chats can be extended with additional knowledge sources.
  • Add knowledge sources to in-app help and guidance for D365 Finance / Supply Chain Management.

KKey concepts

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Finance / Supply Chain
Prebuilt Copilots for F&O agent chats — extensible with knowledge sources for in-app help.
Copilot in Dynamics 365 Customer Experience / Service
Prebuilt agent and seller experiences across CRM scenarios — configurable with business terms and connectors.
M365 Copilot for Sales
Configurable Copilot for sellers tightly integrated with Dynamics 365 Sales / Salesforce.
M365 Copilot for Service
Configurable Copilot for service agents integrated with Dynamics 365 Customer Service / ServiceNow.
Power Platform AI Hub
Central inventory and governance for AI prompts, models, and capabilities across Power Platform.
Knowledge sources for in-app help
Customer-specific docs and policies plugged into D365 SCM / Finance help-and-guidance.

CCheat sheet — when…then

When a customer has D365 → activate the prebuilt Copilots before considering custom.
When sellers use a non-MS CRM → M365 Copilot for Sales still works (Salesforce supported).
When users want better in-app help → add knowledge sources to D365 SCM / Finance help.
When AI sprawl appears in Power Platform → centralize via AI Hub.
When Copilots span multiple D365 apps → design hand-offs across the end-to-end process.
