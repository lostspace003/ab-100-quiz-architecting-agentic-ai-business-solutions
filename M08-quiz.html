RRecap — what to remember

  • Set up monitoring before go-live, not after.
  • Track latency, cost, accuracy, deflection, CSAT, error rate, prompt success.
  • Analyze backlog and user feedback continuously.
  • Use AI-based tools to identify issues — Copilot can review Copilot.
  • Tune topics, prompts, knowledge sources iteratively based on telemetry.

KKey concepts

Telemetry
Topics triggered, fallback rate, latency, escalation, user feedback — the operating signals.
Containment rate
% of conversations resolved without human handoff. Watch with FCR and CSAT or you'll mistake quitting for success.
First-Contact Resolution (FCR)
% of issues resolved on first interaction — better quality signal than containment alone.
Knowledge drift
When source content changes but grounding wasn't refreshed; common cause of post-launch quality drop.
Average Handle Time (AHT)
Service KPI; AHT × volume × loaded cost-per-agent converts to defensible dollar savings.
Tuning loop
Telemetry → identify gap → adjust knowledge / prompts / topics → re-evaluate. Hours, not weeks.

CCheat sheet — when…then

When deflection is up but CSAT is flat → check escalation rate; users may be giving up.
When quality drops 2-4 weeks post-launch → suspect knowledge drift first.
When ROI must be defended → pair telemetry with AHT × volume × loaded cost.
When tuning is needed fast → adjust knowledge / prompts / topics, not the model.
When risk is high → benchmark escalation rate against the task's risk tier.
